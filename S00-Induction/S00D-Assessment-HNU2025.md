## :secret: Task: Enhancement of LLM-Generated Report on Chinese Data Privacy Regulations

With the rise of large language models (LLMs), organizations increasingly use AI to generate draft reports, compliance summaries, and internal policy guidelines. However, raw outputs often lack accuracy, depth, or contextual nuance. This assignment trains you to use LLMs as a **starting point**, and then critically improve upon their work through structured enhancement stages.

---

### Task

1. **Initial LLM Report:**  
   Use a large language model (e.g., DeepSeek, Gemini, ChatGPT or another LLM) to produce a baseline report on related **Chinese Data Privacy Regulations** (e.g., Personal Information Protection Law, Cybersecurity Law, Data Security Law) and their implications for industry or organizational practices.

2. **Improvement Stages:**  
   You will refine and enhance the LLM report step by step. Each stage must:  
   - Identify weaknesses or gaps in the LLM output.  
   - Add improvements (clarity, structure, evidence, examples, or critical analysis).  
   - **Annotate clearly** what has been changed and why  
     *(e.g., ``Improved accuracy by clarifying Article 41 of PIPL and its implications for cross-border transfers``).*  

---

### Stages of Improvement

- **Stage 1 (Minimum for Pass):**  
  Correct factual errors, improve clarity, ensure compliance with basic academic standards.  
- **Stage 2 (Credit level):**  
  Add depth by including specific regulatory details (e.g., case studies, industry guidelines), comparative perspectives, or updated references.  
- **Stage 3 (Distinction level):**  
  Critically analyze implications for industry/organizational practices; propose concrete compliance strategies (e.g., data minimization frameworks, privacy-by-design, cross-border transfer management).  
- **Stage 4 (High Distinction level):**  
  Synthesize insights into a professional report: polish structure, integrate academic and industry sources, and provide forward-looking recommendations (e.g., AI governance, integration with global privacy frameworks like GDPR).  

### Grading Scheme

- **50%** - Quality of baseline LLM report (accuracy, coherence, coverage).  
- **full mark of +10% per improvement stage** (cumulative):  
  - **1 Stage Improved** → 60% (Pass)  
  - **2 Stages Improved** → 70% (Credit)  
  - **3 Stages Improved** → 80% (Distinction)  
  - **4 Stages Improved** → 90-100% (High Distinction, depending on depth & originality)  

---

---

### Evaluation Criteria

- **Accuracy & Completeness** (regulatory details, legal references, organizational implications).  
- **Critical Thinking** (ability to go beyond LLM output, identify gaps, and add meaningful improvements).  
- **Clarity & Structure** (professional academic writing, coherence, logical flow).  
- **Practical Relevance** (applicability of recommendations to real-world industries or organizations).  
- **Annotation Quality** (clear documentation of where and how you improved the report).  

---

### Some potential Ideas

If you have no clear idea, you can consider the following Focus Areas for Industry/Organizational Practice:

- Data collection and consent mechanisms under PIPL.  
- Cross-border data transfer restrictions and compliance strategies.  
- Role of Data Protection Officers (DPOs) in Chinese enterprises.  
- Industry case studies (e.g., e-commerce, healthcare, finance).  
- Comparison with GDPR or other global frameworks.  
- Future challenges (AI governance, algorithmic accountability).  

---

🔑 **Tip:** Treat the LLM as your *"junior assistant"* and yourself as the *"senior analyst."*  
The assignment tests your ability to recognize, critique, and refine imperfect machine-generated work into a professional, value-added output.


### :mailbox_with_mail: Submission

This assignment submission package should include the following three PDF files:

1. **LLM Output Report** (raw, unedited).  
2. **Annotated Improvement Stages** (each stage submitted as a separate version, with highlights and commentary showing what changed).  
3. **Final Enhanced Report** (clean, professional version, without annotations).  

Students are required to submit all 3 deliverables (PDF files) to [unit chair](S00B-Team.md#contacts). The unit chair will reply with an acknowledgement email upon the successful arrival of your file. If you didn't receive any acknowledgement, it means that the submission was not received.
