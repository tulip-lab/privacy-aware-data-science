## Syllabus

The [syllabus](syllabus.md) develops a continuous theory sequence:
privacy attacks, DP definitions and sensitivity, Laplace noise, the
exponential mechanism, Gaussian noise, and composition. Each foundation topic
occupies two 50-minute sessions. The final two sessions introduce PATE and
DP-SGD, one session each.

Worked examples and short calculations accompany the theory. TULIP Lab's
books and papers form the main reading sequence, supplemented by foundational
references and the original algorithm papers.
